# Lemonade stand website (example plan)

Anything before the first `##` heading is ignored.

## Storefront (3 days)

Goal: visitors can see the menu and find the stand.

- **Menu page (M).** Show each drink with a photo and price.
  - AC: every drink has a price and a photo.
  - AC: the page works at phone width.
- **Map and opening hours (S).** Embed a map and list the hours.
  - AC: hours match the sign on the stand.

## Orders (2 days)

Goal: customers can reserve drinks for pickup.

- **Reservation form (L).** Name, drink, quantity, pickup time.
  - AC: invalid quantities are rejected with a clear message.
- **Order list for the owner (M).** A page listing today's reservations.
